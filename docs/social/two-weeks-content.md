## Post 2 — Wednesday · Carousel (4 slides) · Montessori Gently Explained
- **Pillar:** Montessori Gently Explained
- **Format:** Carousel, 4 slides
- **Template:** `tip-montessori` (photo + tip card) for each slide
- **Visual:** Slide 1 photo `transferring.webp`; closing slide photo `classroom-kitchen-view.webp`
- **Per-slide text:**
  - **Slide 1 (cover):** Three small ways to bring Montessori home. No special materials needed.
  - **Slide 2:** Let them pour. A small pitcher and a real glass turn breakfast into practice. Spills are part of the lesson, not a failure.
  - **Slide 3:** Slow down the morning. When a child dresses themselves, it takes longer — and it builds a quiet, lasting confidence. Leave the extra ten minutes.
  - **Slide 4:** Give one real job. Watering a plant or setting the table tells a child: you belong here, and we need you.
- **Caption:**
  > Montessori isn't a shelf of expensive toys. It's a way of seeing your child as
  > capable.
  >
  > Three small things you can try this week, no classroom required. Swipe through, and
  > tell us which one your child takes to.
- **Hashtags:** `#MontessoriAtHome #GentleParenting #Montessori #ToddlerActivities #CapitolHillDC #DCMoms #ChildLedLearning #EarlyChildhoodEducation #MontessoriToddler #DCParents #IndependentChild #WashingtonDC`
- **CTA:** Save this for the weekend, and tell us which one you'll try.
- **Alt text:** Four-slide carousel of simple at-home Montessori ideas, opening with a child transferring objects between bowls and closing with a classroom kitchen view.
