- Gentle scarcity only: "twelve seats," "reserving now," "intentionally small." **No**
  countdown timers, "last chance," or "don't miss out."

- "Children," not "kids." "Guides," not "teachers." "AMI-trained guide," not "best."
- **No emoji.** **No prices** — if pricing comes up in comments or DMs, reply with the
