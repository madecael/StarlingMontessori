# On-image copy — final

Single source of truth for all on-image graphic copy in the Starling social set.

On-image copy is text rendered **on** the graphic. Keep it short and visual — not paragraph captions.

**Voice (non-negotiable):** Gentle. Generous. Grown-up. The brand invites, it does not push. Sentence case. Period-ended lines feel calm and complete. At most one gentle exclamation, only in a CTA. No emoji. "Children" not "kids." "Toddler Community" / "Primary Classroom" / "guides" (not "teachers"). "We/our" for the school, "you/your" for the parent. No hype, no hard sell, no countdown/scarcity pressure (gentle scarcity ok). Never list prices.

> **Do not edit / not generated copy.** The real parent reviews (Hannah, Jennifer) and any Maria Montessori quotes are verbatim source material. They are NOT marketing copy generated here and must stay untouched wherever they appear in the set. Do not paraphrase, trim, or restyle them.
