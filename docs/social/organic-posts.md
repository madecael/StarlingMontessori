# Starling Montessori — 2 Weeks of Organic Instagram Posts

*Soaring minds. Freeing potential.*

Eight feed posts (4 per week) plus six Story prompts, over two weeks. One feed post per week
is a Reel, cross-posted to YouTube Shorts. Every post is locked to a real photo from the
school's cataloged photo bank (`inventario.csv`) — cited by filename, folder, Drive link, and
a one-line description so each pick is verifiable. No photo repeats across the fortnight.

**Voice:** gentle, generous, grown-up. Sentence case. No emoji anywhere. "Children" not
"kids." "Toddler Community," "Primary Classroom," "guides" not "teachers." "We/our" for the
school, "you/your" for the parent. At most one gentle exclamation, only in a CTA. Never list
tuition prices.

**IG handle:** `@starlingmontessori` — **placeholder, confirm before publishing** (flagged
once here; assume correct everywhere below).

**Bio link:** defaults to `/open-house`; switches to `/lp/primary-2026` during the Primary
post (Week 1, Post 4). Set the link in bio per week.

**Start assumption:** week of Monday, June 15, 2026.
