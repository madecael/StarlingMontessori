# Starling Montessori — Social Content System

*Soaring minds. Freeing potential.*

This is the foundation for Starling's organic social presence. It defines what we
post, how often, in what voice, and how each post maps to our branded templates and
photo bank. Everything here is built for **Instagram** (feed, Reels, Stories) as the
primary channel, with **YouTube Shorts** as a secondary home for the same vertical
9×16 Reels. We are intentionally not on Facebook, TikTok, or LinkedIn for this batch.

---

## 1. Brand voice — the short version

Read this before writing anything.

- **Gentle. Generous. Grown-up.** We invite; we never push. No hard sells, no hype.
- **Sentence case.** Period-ended headlines are good — they feel calm and complete.
  At most one gentle exclamation per post, and only in a call to action.
- **No emoji. Ever.** The brand is print-rooted; emoji read as off-brand. Hard rule.
- **Words we use:** "children" (never "kids"), "Toddler Community," "Primary
  Classroom," "guides" (never "teachers"). "We / our" for the school, "you / your"
  for the parent. Use "I" rarely, and only in a genuine founder voice.
- **No hype words:** avoid "game-changing," "revolutionary," "best-in-class,"
  "transform," countdown-timer urgency, or combative anti-objection framing.
- **Tuition — never list prices.** If pricing comes up, use this verbatim:
  > Tuition rates vary based on our program schedules. Please reach out to us
  > directly and indicate the programs you are interested in to receive more
  > information regarding rates & pricing.

## 2. Content pillars (with target mix)

Five pillars. The percentages are the share of feed posts over a rolling month, so
across an 8-post fortnight you will see roughly: 2–3 Prepared Environment, 2 Montessori
Gently Explained, 1–2 Our People, 1 Invitation, and Why We Exist seeded lightly.

### Pillar 1 — The Prepared Environment (~30%)
The calm, the materials, the order. The visual heart of the account: classroom
corners, a child mid-work, the Pink Tower, a set table, the garden walk. This is
"show, don't tell" — the room does the persuading.
- **Templates:** `photo-daily`, plus Reels of the space.
- **Photos:** pink-tower-build, sound-game, transferring, spooning, table-setting,
  classroom-kitchen-view, classroom-stained-glass, stained-glass-arches.
- **Goal:** make a scrolling parent stop and feel the calm.

### Pillar 2 — Montessori Gently Explained (~25%)
One small idea per post: what "follow the child" means, why we don't interrupt
concentration, why the materials are real glass and wood. Plus Montessori quotes.
We teach without lecturing — the parent leaves a little wiser, never talked down to.
- **Templates:** `tip-montessori` (photo + parenting-tip card), `quote-seashell`,
  `quote-purple`.
- **Goal:** earn trust and authority; give parents something they can use at home.

### Pillar 3 — Our People (~20%)
Fabienne, the guides, and the families. Founder moments, the "you meet Fabienne
directly" promise, and real parent reviews rendered as quote cards.
- **Templates:** `meet-founder`, `quote-seashell` (with real review text).
- **Photos:** founder-portrait, founder-with-child, reading-group, toddlers-on-bench.
- **Goal:** the human, family-run trust that a tiny school sells on.

### Pillar 4 — The Invitation (~15%)
Tours, the Toddler Community enrolling now, and the Primary Classroom opening August
2026 — twelve inaugural seats. Gentle scarcity, never a countdown.
- **Templates:** `enroll-primary`, `enroll-photo`, `tour-event`; `enroll-story`,
  `tour-story` for Stories.
- **Goal:** convert warm interest into a booked tour. Keep to ~1 in 5 posts.

### Pillar 5 — Why We Exist (~10%, lightly seeded for now)
The brand communicates *what* it does and *how* well, but has not yet articulated its
*why* (Simon Sinek's sense). Fabienne has not answered the WHY questions yet, so we do
**not** fabricate a belief statement. For now we seed this pillar gently — a single
honest, observational line about why this work matters ("Some mornings the most
important thing that happens here is a child choosing their own work for the first
time.") — and leave a clear hook to activate it fully once Fabienne defines her WHY.
- **Templates:** `quote-purple` or `quote-seashell` for a future founder-belief card.
- **Status:** *Activate when the founder's WHY is defined.* See Open Items below.

## 3. Cadence & weekly rhythm

**4 feed posts per week + 2–3 Stories per week. One of the four weekly feed posts is
a Reel**, cross-posted to YouTube Shorts.

| Day | Slot | Pillar | Typical format |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Monday** | The prepared environment | Pillar 1 | **Reel** (cross-post to YouTube Shorts) |
| **Wednesday** | Montessori, gently explained | Pillar 2 | Single image or carousel (tip / quote) |
| **Friday** | Our people & proof | Pillar 3 | Single image (founder or real review) |
| **Weekend** | The invitation | Pillar 4 | Single image (tour / Primary 2026) |

Stories (2–3/week) ride alongside: a behind-the-scenes moment midweek, a re-share of
the week's best feed post, and a soft tour reminder on the weekend. Why We Exist is
woven in roughly once a fortnight inside whichever slot fits, until the pillar is
fully activated.

**The Reel slot:** the Monday Reel is the one piece of motion each week. Reuse the
existing 9×16 promo videos and b-roll (see the two-weeks plan for which cut runs which
week). Always export/upload the same vertical file to YouTube Shorts.

## 6. Posting checklist (run before every post)

- [ ] **Voice check:** gentle, sentence case, "children" not "kids," "guides" not
      "teachers." Reads like an invitation, not a pitch.
- [ ] **No emoji** anywhere in caption, alt text, or on-image text.
- [ ] **At most one exclamation,** and only in the CTA.
- [ ] **Alt text written** — describe the image plainly for screen readers (every post
      in this system ships with alt text drafted).
- [ ] **Link in bio current** — point the bio link at the right destination
      (`/open-house` for tours; `/lp/primary-2026` during the Primary push).
- [ ] **No prices.** If pricing is implied, use the verbatim tuition line only.
- [ ] **Hashtags:** 10–15, mixed across the three groups, local tags first.
- [ ] **Photo variety:** don't repeat the same photo in nearby posts.
- [ ] **Reels:** export the same 9×16 file to YouTube Shorts.
